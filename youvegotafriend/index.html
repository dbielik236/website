You've Got a Friend

The Music and Stories of Carole King and James Taylor

Spend an evening revisiting the songs you grew up with and discovering the stories you've never heard.

You've Got a Friend is more than a tribute concert. It's an evening of timeless songs, fascinating stories, and genuine connection that celebrates the music of two of the greatest singer-songwriters of all time.

Through live performances and engaging storytelling, Gus & Jean bring audiences behind the music—sharing the friendship between Carole King and James Taylor, the stories behind beloved songs, and the lasting impact they've had on generations of listeners.

As the room fills with songs everyone knows by heart, strangers become a chorus, smiles spread from row to row, and the music becomes something everyone shares together.

About Gus & Jean

Gus & Jean are an award-winning husband-and-wife acoustic duo from New Hampshire whose performances are built around warm harmonies, genuine connection, and a love of sharing music with others.

Having performed together for over two decades, they have entertained audiences throughout New England with shows that are as engaging between the songs as they are during them. Their relaxed chemistry and heartfelt performances create an atmosphere that feels welcoming from the very first note.
