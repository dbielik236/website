Short Bio:

Gus & Jean are a dynamic husband-and-wife acoustic duo bringing fresh energy to classic hits and contemporary favorites. Based in New England, they captivate audiences with their creative interpretations, seamless harmonies, and undeniable chemistry. From James Taylor to Ed Sheeran, their music resonates with listeners of all ages.

Long Bio:

Performing together for over 25 years, Gus & Jean are a New England based husband-and-wife duo, known for their creative interpretations, seamless harmonies, and undeniable chemistry. From classic hits to contemporary favorites, their wide-ranging setlist spans James Taylor to Whitney Houston to Ed Sheeran, connecting with listeners of all ages.

Featured in both the NH Union Leader and The Hippo Press in 2025, their dynamic sound has also earned them a growing social media following including attention of industry figures, including Grammy-winning artists James Taylor and Lisa Loeb.

Whether captivating large crowds on the 92.5 The River stage at the Palace Theatre's Citywide Arts Festival, bringing their signature sound and energy to venues like The Shaskeen or creating intimate vibes at private parties and receptions, Gus & Jean deliver performances that leave audiences feeling the groove long after the last note.
